HOW TO PLAY

  • The bastion fires on its own. You never aim. Your job is deciding what to buy.
  • Coins drop from kills and wave clears. Spend them in UPGRADES during the run — they vanish when the run ends.
  • Cores come from ascending. Spend them in the LAB on permanent bonuses that make every future run stronger.
  • Runs are meant to end. Enemy health grows exponentially; your run upgrades do not. When you stall, ascend and come back harder.
  • Every 10th wave is a boss. Every 25th pays a milestone bonus.
  • Abilities unlock in the Lab and sit at the bottom of the screen. Tap them, or press 1–5.
  • It idles. Leave it running and collect, or play actively with abilities and fast buys. Progress saves automatically.
